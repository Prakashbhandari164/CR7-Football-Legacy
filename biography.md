# Cristiano Ronaldo — Career Overview

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese footballer widely recognized for his athleticism, goalscoring, professionalism and longevity at the highest levels of the sport.

Born in Madeira, Portugal, Ronaldo developed through Sporting CP before making his breakthrough at Manchester United. He later played for Real Madrid and Juventus, returned to Manchester United, and continued his professional career with Al-Nassr. He has also been a defining figure for Portugal's national team.
